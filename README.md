# Lyka Sumaoang | Personal Portfolio

A personal portfolio website developed for the IIDD 2 Final Course Project. This website presents my academic background, technical skills, UI/UX design interests, and selected projects as a Computer Technology student.

## Live Website

[Visit My Portfolio](https://lyka-sumaoang-portfolio.netlify.app/)

## About Me

I am a Computer Technology student at Bulacan State University – College of Industrial Technology, with an interest in UI/UX design and website development. I enjoy creating clean, organized, and user-friendly digital interfaces through design and prototyping.

## Featured Projects

### 1. BulSU CIT Amplified Organizations
A centralized website showcasing the student organizations under the College of Industrial Technology, including their profiles, activities, events, and achievements.

### 2. BulSU Student Portal Redesign
A UI/UX redesign concept for the BulSU Student Portal, focusing on a cleaner dashboard and a more organized enrollment experience.

### 3. YouTube Dashboard Redesign
A dashboard redesign concept that introduces a Continue Watching section and improves access to subscribed channels.

### 4. CITLabCare
A computer laboratory management and repair tracking platform concept designed to help manage equipment records, repair requests, and maintenance progress.
